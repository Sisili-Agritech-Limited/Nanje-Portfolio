Background

Engineering for a
Sustainable Africa

I am an Environmental and Biosystems Engineer with over twelve years of progressive responsibility spanning academia, research, consultancy and professional engineering across government, industry and development projects in Kenya and Sub-Saharan Africa.

My expertise spans agricultural mechanization, irrigation and drainage engineering, renewable energy systems, environmental sustainability, biomedical informatics and climate-resilient infrastructure development, with a focus on the water–energy–food nexus.

I have completed my doctoral studies at the University of Nairobi with a thesis on modelling and performance optimization of a mechanized potato harvester for small and medium-scale farms in Kenya, integrating soil–tool interaction modelling, finite element analysis and multi-criteria decision modelling.

As a NEMA-registered Lead Expert, I have undertaken extensive consultancy on major infrastructure including the Umba Irrigation Project, Galana–Kulalu Food Security Project and several small hydropower and wind power projects. I am also the founder of Sisili Agritech Ltd — a climate-focused agritech enterprise for smallholder systems in East Africa.
