Background

Engineering for a
Sustainable Africa

I am an Environmental & Biosystems Engineer with 12+ years of applied experience across academia, research and consultancy in Kenya and the wider region. My work focuses on engineering solutions that increase smallholder productivity while reducing environmental impact.

Core technical strengths include agricultural mechanization, smart irrigation, renewable energy integration and climate-resilient infrastructure, all centred on the water–energy–food nexus.

My doctoral research at the University of Nairobi combined soil–tool interaction modelling, FEA and field trials to optimise a mechanized potato harvester for small and medium-scale farms. I am a NEMA-registered Lead Expert and founder of Sisili Agritech Ltd, which translates research into operational prototypes and services for farming communities.
